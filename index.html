What makes it different

Ten things no cloud assistant can promise

Loupe is not a chatbot. It doesn't write about your data — it makes typed decisions (yes/no, pick one, score) with a small model that runs where your data already is.

01

Judgments in plain words, run on the device

Write a question the way you'd ask a friend. Laya — a ~320M-parameter decision model — turns it into a yes/no, pick-one or score decision and runs it on your phone or Mac. It doesn't generate text; it decides.

~10 decisions/s on iPhone
02

Nothing leaves the device

Your photos, files, calendar and contacts are read on the device and never sent to us. No account, no analytics. Airplane mode: everything still works.

0 bytes out
03

It shows what it's unsure about — and when a keyword search beats it

You only review what it's torn on. Every judgment is measured against the dumb version — a keyword rule — and if the rule wins, Loupe says so and offers to use it.

baseline check on every judgment
04

Cross-source decisions

One judgment spans everything: “receipts I'll need at tax time” returns email attachments, photographed paper, PDFs and spreadsheet rows in one list.

photos · files · mail · calendar · contacts · web
05

Watchers

Price rises (+23% insurance at renewal), expiring documents, impersonation (“Mum” from an address or number that isn't hers), fake sites and phishing — each with its evidence.

5 watchers
06

Verifiable results — “Show where”

Every result names the item, its source and date, with Open original. Privacy findings re-find the match on tap and show where it is, masked.

no black boxes
07

The privacy check

Finds ID and passport numbers, card numbers, IBANs, keys and tokens, contact lists, payroll sheets and duplicate files in your own sources. Masked previews; nothing proposed happens until you approve.

rules on the device, no model needed
08

Live run view, with the mascot as the engine

Watch a job run: stages on a loop, question diamonds with their latest answer, one particle per real item, who answered, and the cost of asking a cloud model instead.

EN + AR, mirrored in RTL
09

Riverflight — watch the model decide live

A river shooter where Laya flies, with its raw probability bars on screen, against a dumb autopilot on the same seed.

60 fps · p50 ~63 ms
10

Writing assistant: opt-in, your key, drafts only

Off by default. Bring your own provider key (or Ollama on your network) and it drafts replies and second opinions — labelled drafts that wait for your approval. Judgments never depend on it.

never sends anything as you

        Privacy you can check

        0 bytes out. Turn on airplane mode — it still works.

        Laya, the decision model, runs on your iPhone or Mac. Your photos, files, calendar and contacts are read there and are not sent to us or anyone else. No account. No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

        • Every source is off until you turn it on — and the system asks first.
        • Online extras (mail, web templates, phishing lists, the writing assistant) are off by default, labelled Online with their source and time, and each has its own switch.
        • Your keys stay in the Keychain on your device.
